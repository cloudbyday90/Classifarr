captures the source action and requires that action when consuming its private
construction source. The owned builder clears inherited context/advisory for
both actions. A genuinely adopted select candidate receives the exact current
ranking, fresh policy overall confidence and loaded leading library under a
frozen outer context. Unavailable proof leaves null; no new AI outcome is created.

and applied once at actual candidate establishment. Existing exact result/item/
pool/stage/gate/candidate checks, native/compatibility provenance, revision and
active/media-compatible leader checks remain. Missing, copied, offline, changed,
cross-action, overflowing or hostile evidence cannot populate context.
The closed numeric witness keeps its 64-candidate / 64-library / 16-KiB limits;
these are not a size cap on every detail reachable through ranking references.

Baseline objects, candidate identity, outcome confidence, top-level signals/RAG,
existing fallback choices and restrictive authority remain intact. Comparison,

existing no-verification short circuit. Scoring, accounting, provider observations,
retry/cancellation/budget/cooldown behavior and routing/learning permission are
unchanged. Confirm behavior and its current fulfilled verification outputs retain
their existing contracts; automatic/manual and historical paths stay separate.

This is construction-owned context, not continuing freshness. Exact ranking and
library references remain mutable. Later mutation can leave context populated
while the independent v2 review frame correctly becomes a gap. No terminal,
rollout or writer-time refresh/nulling, receipt reassociation or gap upgrade was
added.

## Persistence and review

The full context is not stored wholesale. Removing its earlier nested signals,
RAG and related-evidence summary removes those existing persistence/builder
fallbacks. Explicit top-level evidence preserves its normal precedence and values.
The saved advisory now projects to null, rather than displaying an earlier
candidate's legacy advisory notice. New answer fingerprints and Discord custom
IDs consequently change when that earlier notice had been present.

The v2 frame, technical destination/score, mandatory selection, permitted
candidate commands and no-primary/bulk behavior remain independent. Original
automated-decision metadata is preserved. Stale pending answers fail, while exact
completed receipts recover before new validation and do not create another route.
