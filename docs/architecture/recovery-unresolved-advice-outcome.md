## Recommendations and tradeoffs

The design records the official AWS, W3C and OWASP sources retrieved through MCP,
their applicability, and the alternatives table. Neutral advice removes the
positional guess and empty-pool throw through the existing application process.
It leaves a required choice unresolved rather than creating missing evidence;
it does not improve or certify classification accuracy.

Recommendation stack:

1. Qualify the neutral no-suggestion recovery result with current gates.
2. Investigate the separate policy-signal suggestion's exact destination binding
   and provenance before changing its advice or ranking behavior.
3. Keep unknown side effects outside retry/replay authority; address existing
   runtime-security gaps before later publication qualification.
4. Retain [PR 589's compiler deferral](client-compiler-pr-589-outcome.md) until
   a changed artifact passes the established differential controls. This review
   found the same immutable candidate, no applicable subset and no genuinely
   stale Dependabot branch. No PR merge/rebase or package edit was manufactured.
