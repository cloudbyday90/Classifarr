## Agreed first consumer

Cover new deterministic `prompt_confirm` policy candidates from a genuine
successful adopted reevaluation that remain selected when AI verification is
skipped by an existing gate or genuinely fails. Valid current policy evidence
should supply the existing review frame; missing or changed proof should supply
an explicit gap. Neither outcome establishes that AI ran or confirmed anything.

The current reader can recommend original A/80 after B has been adopted with
fresh overall confidence 75 and candidate score 70. Preserve the retained
outcome's existing maximum-confidence behavior while displaying the fresh policy
mechanics from their own source. This can change pending recommendation, score,
threshold explanation, cards, comparison and presentation-based fingerprints.

Keep policy-only `auto_classify` and `prompt_select`, failed/skipped reevaluation,
initial/non-adopted/generic callers and historical rows separate. Their action or
source contracts need their own assessment.

## Evidence and lifecycle

Reuse the existing live policy witness and frozen seed captured immediately
after successful evaluation, before adoption callbacks. Bind the exact typed
item, policy result/native revision or compatibility origin, loaded pool,
successful stage/gate and actual deterministic candidate, including retained
destination/method/confidence. This proves the loaded snapshot; it does not add
a database freshness lease.

Factor policy-current validation separately from the fulfilled-verification
observer's invocation-context check. A retained policy candidate must not need
an invocation token, provider permission, label-prefix admission or optional
advice/profile to prove policy evidence. The observer remains diagnostic-only
and cannot admit additional AI work.

Associate review ownership with the actual new adopted policy candidate before
later verification gates, or at a bounded equivalent owner boundary. Register an
attempted gap when scoped evidence cannot be established. Retain exact stage and
candidate identity, and latch any observed source loss rather than allowing a
later restoration to upgrade that same receipt.

Existing missing-input and trusted freshness-refusal paths deliberately force
gaps. Their later gap must override earlier positive policy evidence, even when
the separate label-free policy witness remains valid. Fulfilled verification
results keep their existing independent frame behavior.

Publish only after exact final apply selection and fulfilled rollout. Shadow,
comparison/conflict rejection and failed owner processing cannot publish this
candidate as the accepted review source. Reuse existing copy transfers, writer
entry/post-derivation/pre-INSERT checks and canonical row-ID binding in the
existing transaction.

## Storage, disclosure and operator behavior

Reuse `policy.current_review.v1`: complete numeric ranking up to 64 candidates,
up to five closed detail projections and 64 KiB. Keep the numeric witness's
64-candidate/64-library/16-KiB bounds. Preserve explicit unknown purpose evidence
when genuine immutable purpose capture is absent. Add no raw rule, diagnostic
body, provider prose, title, credential or new private-data disclosure.

The saved frame remains with its History row, survives configuration restore
only with that row, and cascades with History deletion. Preserve original
`metadata.policyResult` and the existing portable-backup boundary. Add no schema,
capture version, job, expiry process or historical rewrite.

The existing shared readers must require the retained leader to belong to the
question's permitted destinations. A present invalid frame never revives original
policy evidence. A gap removes the recommended destination and score; existing
explicit manual commands and route-safety status keep their own meanings.
Completed-answer receipt recovery remains before fresh validation. New records'
fingerprints and Discord button identifiers may change; exact completed replay
must remain idempotent.

## Prospective verification status decision

The separate recommended correction is to clear only inherited
`candidate_bound_verification` on a newly constructed adopted prompt-confirm
policy candidate. Current fulfilled provider output would later supply its own
status through existing result construction. Preserve the original baseline
object and all other inherited context/advisory/authority fields.

This prevents an earlier status-only confirmation from appearing alongside the
new policy candidate. It also intentionally changes future History-derived
verification counts and abstention telemetry: retained rows without a current
verification outcome should not count inherited statuses. Genuine provider
capability/transport observations and charged invocation counts remain separate
and unchanged. Historical rows/readers remain unchanged. No new public status,
schema or UI element is needed. The user confirmed this prospective correction.

Clearing authority profiles is outside scope: an inherited `canRoute:false`
remains a routing prohibition. Broader retained context/advisory cleanup is also
outside this first batch.

## Failure, authorization and verification

Keep ordinary skip counts, genuine transient/permanent error classification,
resilience behavior, provider observations, retries, existing budgets/caps,
comparison/rollout and routing/learning authorization. Diagnostic failure must
not replace a primary operation error, grant a route or trigger another call.
No new query, provider request, endpoint, setting, operator CLI trigger or page.

Use production evaluator/owner controls for cap, resilience, mode refusal and
genuine transient/permanent rejection. Verify valid source versus copied,
missing, overflowing and mutated proof, same/different destination, source loss
and restoration, exact candidate retention and forced-gap precedence. Preserve
all four fulfilled verification statuses and excluded action paths.

Use loopback HTTP for real transport/operation failures, charged attempts,
completed observations and error precedence. Use isolated production-factory
PostgreSQL for actual insert/load, BIGINT binding, writer waits, rollback,
concurrency, lost acknowledgement, original/current evidence, stale-answer
refusal and completed replay. Exercise both Discord paths and existing web
contracts. Verify prospective metric exclusion and
unchanged actual provider metrics, including unchanged historical rows.

Run fresh backend coverage and affected quality/security gates. Reuse client
evidence only after its source, installed graph and reports are verified
unchanged; run affected browser checks. Maintain verified development artifacts,
update the separate observed outcome and Unreleased, commit/push on main and
inspect that commit's own CI. No branch, release, deployment or live write.

## Recommendation stack

Prefer genuine retained policy evidence or an explicit gap over absent legacy
fallback or unconditional evidence loss. Keep policy provenance, AI invocation,
actual provider outcome, final apply selection and route authorization distinct.
Reuse the existing bounded frame, publisher, transactional writer, shared readers
and replay machinery. A positive frame preserves useful mechanics; the cost is
new review fingerprints and careful lifecycle validation. Prospective status
clearing removes false confirmation/counting at the cost of intentionally
changing new History-derived statistics.

There are no open PRs at this intake, so no independent dependency batch is
included. Both behavior boundaries were confirmed before implementation.
