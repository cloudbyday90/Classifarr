# Retained adopted policy review outcome

November 13, 2026 (America/New_York). Implemented on main. Design:
[retained policy evidence](retained-adopted-policy-review-design.md); research and
the original reproduction are in the separate
[investigation](retained-adopted-policy-review-investigation.md).

## Result

A new adopted prompt-confirm policy candidate now owns its current review
evidence before optional AI verification gates. A skipped or genuinely failed
verification can retain that evidence, independently of provider availability.
Missing, copied, overflowing or changed proof records an explicit gap instead of
allowing the original policy recommendation to reappear.

The existing small adopted-review service separates policy-current validation
from the fulfilled observer's invocation-context validation. It reuses the live
numeric witness and immediately frozen seed, actual stage/gate/candidate
association, typed item, loaded pool and retained destination/method/confidence.
Observed policy loss latches; restoring values does not upgrade that receipt.
Inspection failure registers a gap without calling accessors or coercion hooks.

Existing invocation freshness refusals still replace earlier policy evidence
with a forced gap. Fulfilled current verification outcomes retain their own
observer and status. Neither positive policy evidence nor its observer grants
permission for AI generation, routing or learning.

The final publisher still requires exact apply selection and fulfilled rollout.
Shadow or rejected candidates cannot publish. Persistence rechecks through the
existing lifecycle waits and binds the actual History ID in the same INSERT.
Shared pending-review, score/card/comparison, web/bulk and Discord readers retain
their existing bounded frame and completed-receipt behavior.

## Prospective verification status

Constructing a new prompt-confirm policy candidate clears only inherited
`candidate_bound_verification`. Current fulfilled verification can supply its
own status afterward. The baseline object, historical rows, other inherited
context/advisory fields and restrictive authority remain unchanged.

Retained rows therefore do not display an earlier candidate's confirmation or
add its status to future History verification counts and abstention telemetry.
Actual provider observations and charged invocation counts keep their separate
meaning. An HTTP stream retry remains two transport attempts within one charged
AI invocation, with existing admission-failure and final observation behavior.

Retained outcome confidence still uses the existing maximum of earlier and
current confidence. Review mechanics use their own current policy values: in
the reproduction, B can retain outcome confidence 80 while its fresh overall
policy confidence is 75 and its displayed candidate score is 70. The frame
does not claim AI agreement, classification accuracy or causal explanation.

## Security, maintenance and delivery boundaries

Full npm audits show zero root/client findings and the same five low-severity
backend tooling findings involving Jest/deepmerge. The existing narrowly scoped
rejected-CVE triage remains unchanged; this batch adds no scanner exception.

scheduled job, operator CLI trigger, historical inference or live write is added.

PRs are not reused as new work. Development maintenance preserves recovery assets,
current evidence, worktrees, toolchains, volumes, services and shared cache.
Final maintenance confirms seven unchanged existing containers, 473 preserved
image/tag entries and four worktrees. No eligible stale artifact was retired.
No release or deployment is performed. Exact-source CI is a separate delivery
check; local results are not a substitute for those receipts.

## Next recommendation

Assess ownership of the retained prompt-confirm result's context and advisory.
Those fields intentionally retain earlier values in this batch; the saved review
frame and cleared verification status now have their correct independent source.
Use a separately agreed boundary before changing those result fields or their
fingerprints. Policy-only select/automatic review evidence remains a later
action-contract extension. Preserve the distinction between policy provenance,
AI operation, final selection and routing authorization throughout.
